How to Use This Book

1
Study 12 words a day. Read the word, the meaning, and the example sentence out loud. Tap the word to hear it in the app version.
2
Check the extras. ≒ means a synonym (similar word), ↔ means an antonym (opposite). Learn them together — they double your vocabulary.
3
Write MY SENTENCE. Make your own sentence for each word. This is how a word becomes YOURS. Say it to your teacher in class.
4
Take the Daily Test. Sections A–D check your memory. Section E is real writing — use three or more of today's words.
5
Review three times. Check a review box each time you come back. Three checks = mastered.
