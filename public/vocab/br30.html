📖 How to Use This Book

1
Look & Listen. Look at the word, the picture, and the meaning. Listen and repeat after your teacher. (단어와 그림을 보고, 선생님을 따라 읽어요.)
2
Read the example. Read the example sentence out loud — big voice! (예문을 큰 소리로 읽어요.)
3
✏️ MY SENTENCE. Make your OWN sentence with the word and write it on the line. Say it to your teacher! (그 단어로 나만의 문장을 만들어 쓰고, 선생님께 말해봐요. 이 책에서 제일 중요한 부분!)
4
Check the boxes. Each time you review a word, check one small box. Three checks = the word is yours! (복습할 때마다 체크! 3번 체크하면 내 단어!)
5
Daily Check. After studying, try the test page. Don't worry about mistakes — trying is winning! (테스트 페이지로 확인해요. 틀려도 괜찮아요!)
