01 · Overview

One student. A separate record for every visit.

Verify student→Link visit→Assign teachers→Continue care

A student keeps one learner record. Each training visit has its own period. A return visit is added without replacing the previous visit.

FeatureCurrent scope
Student reviewAdministrators compare booking entries with the student register and confirm identity before linking.
Visit historyView separate visits with their start and end dates.
Care teamAssign or remove multiple teachers per visit and retain the change history.
Learning experienceTry My Tree House: story, listening, vocabulary, matching, spelling, handwriting and recording.
Still in development

Visit-specific official levels and textbooks, lesson notes, student report publication, child selection, account-based learning sync, recording submission to teachers, feedback and student-linked video reviews are not available yet. Integration with existing class and video-class tools is a later step.

02 · Korean administrators

From student verification to teacher assignment

Sign in → 학생 케어 · 방문 이력 → 학생 연결 검토

  1. Find the student by name or booking reference.

    Compare the names and identifiers in the booking and register. A matching name alone does not establish identity.

  2. Check the review status.

    Only ‘번호 일치 후보’ (matching identifier candidate) supports confirmation at present. Name-only matches, conflicts and missing matches need source review first. Do not register placeholder entries as students.

  3. Choose a new learner or an existing returning learner.

    For a return visit, compare the student's identity and previous dates before choosing the existing learner. If only the name matches, stop and clarify the identity.

  4. Confirm this visit's dates.

    Enter the start and end date, check the identity confirmation box and save. This does not overwrite the original booking or student row.

  5. Assign a teacher to the correct visit.

    Open Student Care, locate the visit and select a teacher under ‘담당 선생님 추가’. Check the student, dates and teacher, then select ‘배정 저장’. Only active local-teacher accounts can be added.

Illustration only — not a real student

If Alex visits in January and returns in July, both visits remain under Alex. Assigning Teacher A to July does not change January's assignments.

When teachers change

Add the new teacher to the visit and remove the previous teacher with ‘배정 해제’ when appropriate. More than one teacher may share care. Removal does not delete the student or visit. Administrators can open ‘담당자 변경 이력’ to see who changed the assignment and when.

Use ‘담당자 미배정 방문’ to find visits without an active, available teacher. Assignments left on inactive accounts also need attention.

Care can continue after departure

Access does not automatically end on the visit end date. Administrators remove the assignment when the teacher's responsibility ends.

03 · Local teachers

Find your students in My Students

Staff sign-in → Teacher Hub → My Students

  1. Search by student name and check the visit start and end dates.
  2. Check the Care team to see the teachers assigned to the visit.
  3. If a student or visit is missing, ask an administrator to check your assignment. Include the exact student and visit dates.

You can currently view only visits assigned to you. Other visits for the same student and the full student register are not shared automatically. Handover of previous-visit reports is still in development.

Local teachers cannot confirm student links or change assignments. The new Student Care page does not display booking fees, settlement details or parent contact information.

If an assignment is removed or your account is deactivated

Access ends on the next request. Use Refresh when the information appears out of date.

04 · Learning experience

Look, listen, write and speak within a story

Mode selection → 학습모드 (Learning) → 나무집으로 출발

The entrance offers Guest (applications) and Learning modes. Guest links to the existing application and booking flow. Focus this test on learning and student care; do not create real payments or applications.

  1. Follow My Tree House.

    Tap objects and scenes to move through the story and listen to English dialogue. The current version follows Bobby and Mia with illustrated scenes and scene motion. The full Dreamy-led game redesign is still in development.

  2. Try the word activities.

    Use listening, word finding, matching and spelling, then return to the story. English playback has been slowed for young beginners. Voices vary with the device's English speech settings.

  3. Write and record.

    Use a finger or pen on the handwriting pad. Record a word or sentence and play it back. Allow microphone access when the browser asks.

Recordings are not submitted to teachers yet

Progress, handwriting and recordings are stored in the current browser on this device. They do not sync across accounts or devices. Clearing browser data may remove them. Handwriting and pronunciation are not automatically graded. Use recordings without personal information during testing.

Official stage labels are DSL-F2, DSL-T1, DR-F1, DR-F2, DR-S and DR-T. The first trial unit is DSL-F2 Week 1.

05 · Troubleshooting

What to do when something goes wrong

IssueWhat to check
Sign-in reminderUse the page's sign-in-again button. The server session may have expired even if an older page is open.
Sign-in service unavailableRetry shortly. If it continues, tell your administrator the time and test URL. Never include your password in messages or screenshots.
Student missingAdministrators should check the confirmed link. Teachers should check their assignment for that visit. Clear the search and filters too.

Save result unavailableYou may retry the same save from the current screen. After refreshing, inspect the history before making another change.

06 · Test together

Complete these checks with test accounts

  • Administrator and local teacher sign-in both work.
  • A test student can be linked and a return visit added without replacing the first visit.
  • A teacher sees assigned visits and cannot see unassigned visits.
  • After removal and Refresh, the visit disappears from the teacher's list.
  • Story, word games, handwriting, recording and playback work.
  • Buttons and fields are usable on a phone.

When reporting an issue, include the URL, device and browser, steps taken, expected result and actual result. Do not include passwords or real students' personal information.
