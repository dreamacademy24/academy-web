Print on A4 at 100% / Actual size. Choose double-sided printing and flip on the long edge. First test pages 1–2. Cut along the guides, then laminate and leave a 3–5 mm sealed border. Each card is 90 × 80 mm. Front and back have the same card number.

Start with 3–5 words. Listen, point, say, then turn over. Book sets follow the vocabulary in the reference photos; extra sets add family, colors, numbers and everyday words. Instructions and meanings are in English. Chinese uses simplified characters and tone-marked pinyin.

Audio: Mandarin synthetic speech. Stars are saved only in this browser on this device; they mark practice, not pronunciation scores. No account, microphone or recording is required.
